Drawer 08 · FAQ

Questions people ask before booking

Do I need to be home during the session?

For the first hour, yes, so we decide together what stays. After that you can leave. Many clients go to work and come back to a finished space.

Will you throw my things away?

Never without you. You make every keep / donate / discard decision. I only make it faster by sorting everything into categories first.

Do I need to buy boxes and organisers?

Usually not for the first session. I work with what you have. If something would really help, I suggest it, you approve it, and I buy it at actual cost with the bill.

What areas do you cover?

All of Hyderabad and Secunderabad. Inside the Outer Ring Road, travel is included. Beyond ORR, it's ₹15 per km each way, shown on the receipt before you book.

How is this different from a cleaning service?

Cleaners make a space clean; I make it work. We decide what you own, where it lives, and how it stays that way. Light dusting happens as a side effect.

Do you do offices or kids' rooms?

Kids' rooms, yes, and they're some of my favourites. Small home offices, yes. Commercial offices, not right now.

How do I pay?

UPI or bank transfer. 20% to confirm the date, the rest on the day after the work is done.
